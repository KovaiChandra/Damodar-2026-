The Sacred Month

What is Damodar Month?

Damodar Month — called Kārtika in the Vaishnava calendar — is the holiest month of the year, observed from late October through November. Its name recalls the day Mother Yashoda, in loving anger at her mischievous son, tied Him around the waist with a rope. Dāmodara means "He who was bound around the abdomen." The Lord, who cannot be held by any force, let Himself be bound by one thing alone — a mother's pure, selfless love.

During this month, devotees offer a ghee lamp each evening and sing the Damodarashtakam — eight sacred verses glorifying this divine pastime. The Padma Purāṇa declares that a single lamp offered in Kārtika erases the sins of many births.
